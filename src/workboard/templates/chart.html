How this is counted
  • Completion is rows in the board's Done table divided by all rows. Every row counts the same, whatever its size.
  • Where the board still lists a row that was split into parts, the parent and its parts are each counted.
  • "Ready to start" means the row is unclaimed and everything in its Needs column is in Done. "Waiting on other rows" means at least one need is still open.
  • A finished row filed under two sections is counted in the first.
